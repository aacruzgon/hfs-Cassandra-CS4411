# ADR-aacruzgon-0060: Step 3's scope: vread, list_versions and delete_with_match come forward from Step 4, PATCH is in, the multitenancy suites split, and four contradictions are realigned; Steps 4 to 8 left to the group (**X**)

- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Status:** accepted
- **Context:**
    - **The race suite needs Step 4 methods.** Roadmap Step 3's exit criterion and row 3.3 require every test of `search/versioned_write_race_suite.rs`. Its bound is `ResourceStorage + VersionedStorage`, and it calls `list_versions` in all five tests, `vread` in two and `delete_with_match` in one (Step 3 plan D1). All three were Step 4 stubs, and row 4.1 put `VersionedStorage` at Step 4.
    - **The versioned `Location` would point at a 501.** From Step 3, every create, update-as-create and revive answers `Location: …/_history/N` (ADR-aacruzgon-0049). With vread still a stub, every one of those links would answer 501, and `/metadata`'s `readHistory: true` would be false (plan D19; matrix V-6, CS-2). ADR-aacruzgon-0049 had the Milestone A `GET` of that `Location` assert 501 until Step 4.
    - **PATCH goes live with `update`.** `crates/rest/src/handlers/patch.rs` reads, checks `If-Match`, then calls `ResourceStorage::update`, and `/metadata` already lists `patch`. [R4 #patch](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/http.html#patch): "servers SHALL support conditional PATCH, which works exactly the same as specified for update in Concurrency Management". Neither the design's task list nor the roadmap named it (matrix DS-7).
    - **The multitenancy suites were gated twice.** Roadmap section 5 put `tenant_id_fidelity`, `tenant_existence` and `multitenancy_suite` at Step 3; progress row 6.5 put them at Step 6. Some of their tests need methods Cassandra does not have at Step 3: `register_tenant`, `deregister_tenant` and `purge_tenant_data`, which the design never gives it, and a real `count_by_tenant`, which waits for row 6.5 (plan D23; ADR-aacruzgon-0040 for `$purge`).
    - **Three more places disagreed** (plan D21, rows 3 to 5):
        - ADR-aacruzgon-0034 `:13` has Step 3 add the G38 `code` bound to `update`, `update_with_match` and `create_or_update`, while roadmap Step 5 still listed that bound as its own task;
        - ADR-aacruzgon-0033 `:12` has Step 3's `update` read `K(N)` from `resources.index_keys`, while roadmap Step 5 still listed that read as its own task;
        - ADR-aacruzgon-0024 (revive by `SET deleted_at = null`, `:37`, `:54`, `:115`, `:145`) and ADR-aacruzgon-0012 (`:13`, V3 at "step 4") are partly superseded by ADR-aacruzgon-0027 (G39, G40), but their index rows still read plain "accepted".
- **Decision:**
    1. **`vread`, `list_versions` and `delete_with_match` are Step 3's** (D1, with D19 following from it), in `crates/persistence/src/backends/cassandra/versions.rs` (layer L2, PR #58):
        - `vread` is R3 on `resource_versions` (walkthrough 4.2); a delete marker comes back with `deleted_at` set and content `Value::Null`, which REST answers 410;
        - `list_versions` is R4, oldest first, numeric past 9;
        - `delete_with_match` takes the client's `If-Match` field value, checks it with `if_match_field_satisfied` against an R1, then calls `delete_versioned` with the bare current version, as SQLite, PostgreSQL and MongoDB do. A plain pass-through would compare `W/"2"` with `2` and always conflict.
        - **Milestone A's `GET` of the create's `Location` answers 200 with version 1 from Step 3** (`crates/hfs/tests/cassandra_milestone_a.rs:408-418`), not from Step 4 as ADR-aacruzgon-0049 says. ADR-aacruzgon-0049 is not edited; its index status gains a pointer here.
        - **Step 4 keeps** `update_with_match`, `current_version` (today the trait default, which calls `read`), `versioning_suite.rs`, `search/conditional_if_match_suite.rs` (which cannot pass on Cassandra as written; what replaces it is for Step 4's ADR, roadmap Step 4's handover) and the `Versioning` declaration, which still waits for `versioning_suite` to pass (ADR-aacruzgon-0037).
    2. **PATCH is in Step 3, with no backend code** (D17). It is `update`. It is tested through REST in `step3_crud_through_rest` (layer L4): JSON Patch and FHIRPath Patch answer 200 with `ETag: W/"N+1"`, a stale `If-Match` is 412 with the resource unchanged, and a PATCH of a deleted resource is 410 and does not revive it.
    3. **The multitenancy split** (D23, D21 row 6). Step 3 runs the two generic tests that need only CRUD: `tenant_id_fidelity_suite`'s `distinct_tenant_ids_never_share_data` and `tenant_existence_suite`'s `child_data_is_not_the_parents` (layer L4, `crates/persistence/tests/cassandra_tests.rs`). Excluded, with the reason in roadmap section 5:
        - `tenant_has_resources_matches_discovery`, which needs the tenant-registry methods and `$purge`;
        - `purging_one_tenant_leaves_the_look_alikes_intact`, which needs `PurgableStorage` (`$purge` is 501, ADR-aacruzgon-0040);
        - `multitenancy_suite.rs`, which is SQLite-only.

       A `count_by_tenant` override, for the Tenants UI and `/admin/tenants`, stays a row 6.5 item.
    4. **The G38 bound** (D21 row 3): `update` and `create_or_update` check it from Step 3 (`update.rs:113`, `upsert.rs:185`); `update_with_match` gets it at Step 4. Roadmap Step 5 keeps only the per-row `param` measurement.
    5. **`K(N)` from R1** (D21 row 4): `update` reads it from Step 3; Step 5 adds the other index tables, not the read.
    6. **The index statuses of 0012 and 0024** (D21 row 5) become "accepted; partly superseded by ADR-aacruzgon-0027 (G40)" and "(G39)". Their files are not edited (rule: append-only).
    7. **`Crud` and `OptimisticLocking` are declared with the suites** (D7), in layer L4's commit that first runs them green. `/metadata` does not read `BackendCapability`: `crates/rest/src/handlers/capabilities.rs` hard-codes its interactions (design, "Capabilities and rejection"). So the declaration changes `Backend::supports()` and the capability contract test, and nothing a client sees.
    8. **The roadmap's Step 3 task list gains** the three methods, PATCH, the delete marker's content (V2: `deleted_at` and `last_updated` the delete's instant, `data` unset, `index_keys` the empty set), the REST test and the multitenancy pair.
    9. **Steps 4 to 8 are not assigned, and their decisions are not made here.** On 2026-11-07 Alan left both to the group: who takes each step from Step 4 on, and each later step's open questions, which that step's group decides in its own ADR while he audits and, where needed, amends them. This is not a team decision; it records that none has been made yet. So the roadmap's "Who" lines for Steps 4 to 8 and the progress matrix's Owner column from row 4.1 on read **X** ("not assigned yet; the group decides"), noted in the matrix legend and in section 4's introduction. **W**, the owner of shared files, and Steps 0 to 3 are unchanged. Where Step 3's work raised a later step's question (Step 4's suite, its mismatch error and `current_version`; Step 5's batching rule), the roadmap's handover gives it as an open item with a recommendation and its evidence, never as decided.
- **Alternatives considered:**
    - **Move the race suite to Step 4's exit criterion** (D1 b): lost. The race suite is the compare-and-swap contract; ADR-aacruzgon-0024 calls it the Step 3 exit criterion. Moving it would leave the riskiest code without its gate.
    - **Merge Steps 3 and 4** (D1 c): lost. Step 4's own gates, `versioning_suite` and 12 versions past 9, are independent of the CAS.
    - **Keep vread's 501 until Step 4 and record V-6 and CS-2 as false claims** (D19 b), or **a 404 stub for one step** (D19 c): lost once D1 made vread real.
    - **Refuse PATCH on Cassandra** (D17 b): lost. REST has no per-backend PATCH gate, so it needs an upstream handler change while `/metadata` keeps listing `patch`. **PATCH in but untested** (D17 c): lost; the SHALL needs a test.
    - **All three multitenancy suites at Step 6** (D23 b): lost; the two CRUD-only tests are about 30 lines and check isolation on the real write path. **All at Step 3 with a `count_by_tenant` override now** (D23 c): lost; that is an admin aggregate with its own cost (a scan of every partition key), and the excluded test needs the registry and `$purge` as well.
- **Consequences:**
    - **Step 4 is small.** What it still has to build is `update_with_match` and, if wanted, a `current_version` override; its suites and the `Versioning` declaration are its gate. The roadmap's Step 4 section says so, and lists what Step 3 left for it (the handoff commit after this one).
    - **Every versioned `Location` resolves** from Step 3: 200 for a content version, 410 for a marker. `readHistory: true` is true from Step 3.
    - **`list_versions` has no REST caller** (only a test mock in `crates/rest/src`); it is in Step 3 for the race suite alone.
    - **The roadmap section 5 row for the three multitenancy suites** now says which two tests run at Step 3, and row 6.5 says what stays at Step 6.
    - **`/metadata` is unchanged by the declarations,** so nobody should expect it to move at Step 3; Step 7 is where it changes (roadmap 7.1, 7.2).
